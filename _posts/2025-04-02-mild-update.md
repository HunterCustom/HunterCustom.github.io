<h2>Current Status</h2>
<p>Right now, the car is in storage, and I've been working on setting up my storage unit to make things more efficient. I installed a battery from a solar system to power shop lights inside and added a diesel heater to help maintain warmth. The Miata itself is up on four 6-ton jack stands, and I've removed several components:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Front bumper</li>
    <li>Front splitter</li>
    <li>Brake ducts</li>
    <li>Rear splitter</li>
    <li>Transmission</li>
    <li>PPF</li>
    <li>Driveshaft</li>
</ul>

<h2>Budget and Plans</h2>
<p>At this point, my progress is largely dictated by my budget. If I just wanted to get the Miata back on the road, it looks like I'd be facing a $3,500 bill. However, since I already have an OS Giken LSD for the car, I'd like to install it, which adds another $210. Ideally, I'd also love to rebuild the front and rear suspension with new tuned coilovers, fresh bushings, and, most importantly, upgraded wheel hubs.</p>

<p>We'll see how things progress, but for now, that's where I'm at. Thanks for following along, and I'll keep you all posted!</p>
